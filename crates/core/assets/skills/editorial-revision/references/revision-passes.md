# Revision Passes

## Structural Pass
- Audience: who must understand or be persuaded?
- Purpose: inform, decide, sell, teach, request, record, or persuade.
- Shape: does the document open with the right promise?
- Argument: are claims supported by reasons or evidence?
- Sequence: does each section answer the reader's next question?
- Missing material: definitions, caveats, examples, counterarguments, next steps.

## Line Pass
- Cut duplicated setup.
- Replace "there is/there are" openings when they hide the actor.
- Move conditions before consequences only when the reader needs the condition first.
- Use parallel structure in lists.
- Prefer specific verbs over adjective-heavy phrasing.
- Vary sentence length for rhythm.

## Tone Adaptation
- Executive: shorter, decision-oriented, explicit risk/tradeoff.
- Academic: precise, scoped, evidence-aware, less promotional.
- Friendly: direct, warm, concrete, less formal scaffolding.
- Sales: outcome-led, specific proof, clear ask, no vague hype.
- Support: empathetic, procedural, unambiguous next step.

## Material Change Labels
Use these labels when explaining edits:
- Clarified: same claim, clearer expression.
- Reordered: same material, different structure.
- Tightened: removed redundancy.
- Added: new claim, example, bridge, or caveat.
- Changed: meaning or emphasis shifted.
- Cut: removed claim or section.
